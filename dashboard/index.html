Methodology
    Honest Caveats
    • Regime dependence is the headline. The desk's strong recent Sharpe comes from the 2020s commodity regime; 2011–2015 (the commodity super-cycle bear) produced five straight losing years. Passive commodities (DBC) lost over half its value in those same years.
    • Rules were designed against research on 2020-2025 data; the pre-2020 window is out-of-sample for the configuration, and it shows a much lower Sharpe.
    • Long/flat only — the strategy cannot short, which is where trend strategies earned much of their return in sustained bear markets.
    • Several ETFs launched mid-backtest (Brent 2010, grains/copper/sugar late 2011); early years trade a narrower universe.
    • No learning, no fitted parameters: every rule is a fixed formula, so results are reproducible and every day shown is out-of-sample.
